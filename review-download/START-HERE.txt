EtalonOps MRF Builder — local preview

Download EtalonOps-preview.zip, extract it to a normal folder such as Downloads,
and double-click EtalonOps-preview.html to open it in Chrome or Edge.
No administrator/root access, Node.js installation, or Azure account is required.

Try the 1-inch PVC and self tappers examples. Enter a quantity unit, add a result,
edit the MRF lines, test delivery-date overrides, and Save as Draft.
The DM button lets you leave/resume the demo using browser-local storage.

All catalog records are fictional fixtures; AI responses are scripted.
Do not enter sensitive information. Drafts stay in this browser. Browser policies
may restrict downloaded HTML or local storage. The PDF provides a static review.

No cloud services have been provisioned and no DNS records have been changed.
